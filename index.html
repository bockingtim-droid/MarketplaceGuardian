How to Spot a Counterfeit

  • Compare the price to the manufacturer's MSRP — a price that's dramatically below market value is the single biggest red flag.
  • Check for stock/catalog photos reused across multiple listings, inconsistent lighting, or missing angles of the actual item.
  • Look closely at logos, fonts, stitching, and packaging for spelling errors, blurry printing, or misalignment.
  • Check the seller's account age, review history, and ratings — new accounts with no history are higher risk.
  • Ask for original packaging, authenticity cards, or serial/batch numbers you can verify with the manufacturer.

Avoiding Online Shopping Scams

  • Pay by credit card, not wire transfer, cash app, or gift cards — those payment methods offer little to no fraud protection.
  • Be skeptical of urgency tactics ("only 1 left!", "sale ends in 10 minutes") designed to rush your decision.
  • Verify the site uses HTTPS and has real contact information, a return policy, and verifiable reviews outside its own page.
  • If a deal seems too good to be true for a brand-name item, it almost always is.
  • Search the seller's name or domain plus the word "scam" before purchasing from an unfamiliar store.

File an Official Complaint

Guided walkthrough for submitting this high-risk listing to ReportFraud.ftc.gov

These steps reflect ReportFraud.ftc.gov's reporting flow as checked on — the FTC can change its form at any time, so treat this as a helpful guide, not a guarantee of an exact match.

Step-by-step

  1. Go to ReportFraud.ftc.gov and click "Report Now".
  2. Choose "Online shopping", then "A problem with an online purchase or sale".
  3. Under "Report details," check "I received a fake/counterfeit product".
  4. If you paid the seller, select "Yes" and enter the amount — copy it from the box below.
  5. For "How did it start," "Website or App" usually fits an online marketplace listing.
  6. When asked for details about the company/individual, choose "Yes" for additional info and paste the Seller & Listing box below into the Comments field.
  7. Paste the Description box below into "Describe what happened."
  8. If the form lets you attach files, upload your screenshots below. Otherwise, keep them for your own records.
  9. Review everything you entered, then submit your report.
